  Vision
  As a school, we seek to awaken in our students their identity as worshippers, from a biblical perspective. From there, we want them to discover and develop their gifts for the Kingdom of God. Our goal is not to form better musicians or artists, but better worshippers: those who worship God in spirit and in truth.
  Purpose
  We seek to correct a mistaken worldview of worship and to be a point of unity among ministries, schools, and churches, providing a shared biblical understanding that allows us to speak the same language and move toward the same goal: Christ.
  The School of Worship belongs to the Faculty of Christian Ministries. Each week covers units such as intimacy, identity, and character of the worship leader; biblical and historical foundations of praise; freedom in the Spirit; the prophetic role of the worshipper; musicality and creativity; worship and spiritual warfare; reaching the nations through worship; and sound formation, among others.
